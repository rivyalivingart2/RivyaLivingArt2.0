## Boundaries and recovery

No schema changes, new dependencies, old-product transfer, scraper, catalogue ingestion, backup work or customer messages. Private files, credentials, raw screenshots, local databases and session information remain outside Git. The local file adapter refuses production and any environment lacking its full loopback isolation guard.

Publication is optimistic-version checked, checks current dependencies and retains immutable history. A failed/uncertain batch must be rechecked; no blind replay or direct SQL content writes. Previously published/hidden records must use individual editors; batch publication never edits their source.

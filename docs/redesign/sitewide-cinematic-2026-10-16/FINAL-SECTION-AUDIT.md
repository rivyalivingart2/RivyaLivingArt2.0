# Final website section audit

16 October 2026. This register retains all **146 section-row identities, in the original order**, from [ROUTE-AUDIT.md](ROUTE-AUDIT.md). That file remains the historical source inventory and target plan. The values here describe the final local implementation and the evidence actually available; they do not certify production activation, every record, every conditional state, or a release.

Fullscreen means the section body, not simply the page-level canvas. **Yes** means a safe 100svh minimum that can grow; **Partial** identifies a viewport opening/film or desktop-only minimum alongside natural content; **No** keeps natural content height. No row requires a fixed viewport box that clips its content. A 100svh Studio shell does not make its tables or forms fullscreen sections.

## Editing and presentation ownership

Six new registered candidates appear in Studio but require an explicit draft and publication. Existing Story, Process, Materials and Architects records gain missing chapters through an idempotent **Add missing detailed chapters** action. The action leaves those additions hidden until reviewed and preserves existing copy, order, translations and crops. Twelve Process sections distinguish request/agreement from making questions. Contact and policy facts retain their current records.

Static images reuse P3's reviewed Drive assets plus three visually checked, already-published product image subjects for journal cover corrections. No original asset or product gallery association changes. P4 does not claim video production, real installations, named staff identity or an unverified fabrication method.

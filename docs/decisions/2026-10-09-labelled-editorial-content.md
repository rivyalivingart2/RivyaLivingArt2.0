# Owner decision: labelled concepts and fictional samples

On 9 October 2026, during the local migration implementation, the owner asked the assistant to create the new content, use suitable Drive/old-site media or create illustrations, and keep copies in both Drive and the Studio database for website display.

Asked to resolve the original genuine-only requirement, the owner explicitly selected: **“Use labelled concepts and fictional samples.”**

This supersedes the genuine-only restriction for the **new** 120 Portfolio and 120 Testimonials targets. It does not reclassify existing records or establish customer consent, completed projects, real quotations, ratings or actual orders.

- New Portfolio records created without real project evidence are **Concept project — illustrative, not a completed commission**.
- New Testimonials created without original feedback are **Fictional sample — not customer feedback**. Use sample identifiers, not invented customer identities, credentials, ratings or verified-purchase badges.
- Carry provenance and the visible label through the Studio list/editor, saved preview, public cards/details, search, selected home/landing blocks and Drive copies. Do not put fictional samples in Review/AggregateRating structured data or use them as genuine social proof.
- Real source-backed entries remain a distinct class and require genuine evidence and permission. Generated images and old-site visuals are illustrations unless independently established otherwise.
- The 120 Journal and 120 FAQ targets still require substantive, distinct copy. Do not invent business capabilities, prices, dates, health/safety claims or guarantees.
- Copy new approved-for-use content/assets into a dedicated Drive delivery folder; preserve the supplied originals and existing sharing. Store the same stable identifiers, provenance and version in the additive Studio records. Database records reference durable media assets; do not put binary media in text columns.

The code/release hold remains: local implementation and isolated Studio QA first; no push, PR, merge or deployment until explicitly requested. Track Drive delivery, local Studio insertion and website publication separately; preparing a draft is not publication. Unperformed native-reader, human, device and real-world checks remain open.

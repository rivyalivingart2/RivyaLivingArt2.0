The chosen uncertainty rule is conservative: mark the workbook inflight before dispatch, atomically commit record/receipt/audit/history plus a committed marker, and block new writes if the outcome cannot be confirmed. Do not infer failure from a lost response or immediate old-state read. This can reduce availability until operator reconciliation, but prevents retries and worker cleanup from overwriting a late commit. Lease expiry is checked at gateway admission; pending provider writes remain fenced. Tests execute the exact gateway source in a local provider emulator, which does not prove Google account permissions or latency.

Keep each mutation, its revision, audit and idempotency receipt in one workbook batch; use protected canonical ranges and validated staging inputs. Cross-workbook changes need explicit staged workflows and recovery, never an invented multi-workbook transaction.
